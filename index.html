How to start using 'Plan My Plate'

  1. Select your meals for each day of the week.
  2. Generate shopping list for the selected meals.
  3. Save your meal plan once you've selected meals for each day.
  4. Repeat the process for each day until you have a complete meal plan for the week.
  5. 'Plan My Plate' will automatically calculate the total prep time needed for the week's meals.
